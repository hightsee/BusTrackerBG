# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Belgrade transit riders using the app outdoors, often one-handed, in daylight, and under time pressure. They may know a stop number, a line number, a stop name, or only an address. The app must also remain usable for older or less technical users who do not understand transit data terminology.

## Product Purpose

BusTrackerBG answers two primary questions quickly: when does my bus arrive, and how do I get there. Success means a user can find departures or a route in under ten seconds without needing to understand maps, GTFS, platform variants, or account features.

## Brand Personality

Direct, calm, practical. The interface should feel like a reliable street tool, not a transport operations dashboard or a promotional landing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid map-first layouts that hide the answer, marketing hero sections, decorative UI, account-gated saving as the default path, technical status copy, and flows where users must configure filters before seeing useful departures.

## Design Principles

- Put the answer before the controls.
- Optimize for known stop numbers and line numbers.
- Use maps as supporting context, not the main task.
- Make saving and repeating common trips effortless.
- Prefer clear language over transit-system terminology.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use large tap targets, high contrast, readable type, and direct labels. Do not rely on color alone for state. Motion should be short and functional. Empty and error states should explain what happened and what to do next.
